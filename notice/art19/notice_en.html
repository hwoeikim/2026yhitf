The Korea Sports Safety Foundation offers Sports Activity Safety Mutual Aid (스포츠활동안심공제), a personal accident cover for sports participants.

The organizer's liability insurance covers only accidents caused by the organizer's negligence. To be covered for personal illness or accidents without organizer negligence, we recommend taking out personal accident insurance or mutual aid on your own. (See Article 19 of the Race Rules.)

Overview

· Eligibility: amateur and professional athletes
· Period: 1 to 30 days
· Fee: KRW 4,090 (1 day) to KRW 24,210 (30 days) (basic plan, domestic, age 15 and over)

Key features

· Can be combined with other personal insurance (liability cover is proportional)
· Covers accidents while traveling (compulsory insurance such as auto or flight applies first)
· No underwriting review: medical history, chronic illness, or disability do not prevent enrollment
· Individual enrollment available (2 or more people for those under 15)

Main coverage

Accidental death and permanent disability KRW 100 million / Medical expenses KRW 5–10 million / Liability KRW 10 million / Personal belongings KRW 500,000 / Fracture surgery KRW 500,000 (domestic)
