Article 19 (Insurance and Liability for Injuries and Accidents)

[Organizer's Liability Insurance]

To prepare for accidents that may occur during event operations, the organizer takes out organizer's liability insurance with the Korea Sports Safety Foundation. However, this insurance covers only the organizer's legal liability arising from the organizer's negligence. Accidents caused by a participant's pre-existing condition that the participant was not aware of, and accidents for which the organizer is not found legally negligent, are excluded from coverage.

[Scope of Compensation and Assessment Criteria]

In the event of an accident, whether insurance benefits are paid and the amount of compensation strictly follow the insurer's terms and conditions, the results of the loss adjustment assessment, and whether legal liability exists. The organizer bears no additional financial or legal liability beyond the insurance terms and assessment results.

[Limitations of Coverage and Exclusions]

Insurance coverage may be limited or denied for accidents caused by a participant's intent or gross negligence, pre-existing conditions (medical history) or chronic illness, physical defects, or failure to understand the race rules and safety instructions, and the participant bears full responsibility for such accidents.

The organizer accepts no responsibility for the loss, damage, or theft of personal belongings during the race.

[Recommendation to Take Out Personal Accident Insurance]

1. Sports Activity Safety Mutual Aid (스포츠활동안심공제): To make up for the limits of the organizer's liability insurance (personal illness, accidents without organizer negligence, etc.), participants are strongly recommended to take out personal accident insurance on their own, such as the Korea Sports Safety Foundation's Sports Activity Safety Mutual Aid.

2. Benefits of personal insurance: With personal accident insurance such as the Sports Activity Safety Mutual Aid, actual losses from an accident, including accidental death and permanent disability, medical expenses, and fracture surgery costs, can be covered, and it can be combined with other personal insurance.

3. Minors and guardian consent: For safety, preschool children and participants under 18 are recommended to take out personal or group accident insurance with the consent of a parent or guardian.
