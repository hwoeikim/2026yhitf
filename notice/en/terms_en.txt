<strong>Race Rules and Participant Agreement</strong>


All participants in the 2026 Yonhap News Hanam International Trail Run Festival must fully understand and agree to the following provisions. Those who do not agree may not participate in the event.


1. Participants must faithfully comply with the race rules established by the organizer and must understand and observe all of the provisions below. Violations may result in restricted participation or disqualification during the race.

2. Participants must not litter or damage the natural environment during the race. Violations may result in disqualification.

3. Participants must be fully aware of the nature and risks of long-distance running and trail running, and must take part only after sufficient training and preparation to participate in and complete the event. Participants must also check their health condition in advance and confirm that they have no physical condition that prevents participation.

4. Unlike ordinary road running, trail running events include mountainous areas and unpaved sections, and risks may arise from terrain, weather, and other conditions. Participants must take part with a full understanding of these characteristics of the event.

5. Participants must judge their own health condition and racing ability before taking part. Accidents arising from a participant&#39;s intent or negligence, a pre-existing medical condition, or a violation of the race rules will be handled in accordance with applicable laws and the insurance terms and conditions.

6. Participants may be photographed or filmed at the event venue, and agree that such material may be used on the website, social media, newspapers, magazines, broadcasts, press releases, and other promotional materials for event records and promotion.

7. If the event is changed or canceled due to a natural disaster, severe weather, restrictions imposed by the relevant authorities, or other circumstances beyond the organizer&#39;s control, the matter will be handled in accordance with the event operation and refund rules announced separately.

8. The organizer may inspect mandatory equipment for participants before the race or for award recipients after the race. Failure to carry the designated mandatory equipment may result in restricted entry, removal from the race, disqualification, cancellation of finisher status, or cancellation of an award. Mandatory equipment will be announced separately.

9. If a participant leaves the designated course, uses a vehicle or other external means of transportation, or receives outside assistance not permitted by the rules, measures such as disqualification or invalidation of the result may be taken.

All participants must fully understand and comply with these rules. In the event of a violation, the organizer may restrict participation, remove the participant from the race, disqualify the participant, invalidate the result, or cancel an award.


<strong>Article 1 (Nature of the Event and Eligibility)</strong>

1. Unlike a conventional road marathon, trail running events are races run on unpaved mountainous terrain.

2. The course may include mountainous areas and steep sections, so participants must fully check the course characteristics of their race category before taking part.

3. Participants in trail running events must meet the following eligibility requirements.

- Be at least 18 years old on race day

- Be in good health, with no high blood pressure, heart disease, or other condition that may interfere with strenuous exercise and restrict participation in the event

- Be able to complete the course within the time limit using a GPS/GPX file or on-site course markings

4. There is no age limit for the running events (5KM · 10KM). However, personal or group insurance is recommended for preschool children.

5. For participant safety, the organizer and the race office may, where necessary, require certain eligibility criteria or the submission of supporting documents.

6. Participants must decide for themselves whether to take part, considering their own health condition, experience, and racing ability. A medical check-up before the event is recommended.


<strong>Article 2 (Equipment and Familiarity with Its Use)</strong>

1. Participants must prepare appropriate clothing and equipment to protect themselves against changing weather and severe conditions that may occur during the race.

2. Trail running participants must prepare the items and equipment they need during the race by themselves, in accordance with the principle of Self-Sufficiency.

3. The organizer may conduct mandatory equipment checks when necessary. Failure to carry designated equipment may result in restricted entry, removal from the race, disqualification, or cancellation of an award. Mandatory equipment will be announced separately.

4. Participants must prepare by themselves the personal food and supplies they need outside the official aid sections.

5. Mandatory equipment for the trail running events follows the Trail Run Mandatory Gear table on the registration page and in the Notice section of the website. Participants who do not carry the mandatory equipment on race day will not be allowed to start, and the entry fee will not be refunded.


<strong>Article 3 (Personal Responsibility and Safety)</strong>

1. Because trail running events pass through mountainous areas, immediate rescue or medical support may be difficult in an emergency.

2. The organizer may station checkpoints and safety personnel on the course, but it may be difficult to identify immediately what happens in every section.

3. Emergency contact numbers will be provided before the event. Due to the nature of mountainous terrain, communication may be unreliable in some sections.

4. Participants must put their own health and safety first during the race. If any physical problem occurs, they must stop racing immediately and ask race staff or medical personnel for help.


<strong>Article 4 (Environmental Protection)</strong>

1. Environmental protection is an important operating principle of this event.

2. Participants must not discard waste generated during the race on the course and must carry it to a designated location for disposal.

3. Littering or intentional damage to the natural environment, if confirmed, may result in disqualification.

4. Participants in trail running events must carry a personal cup. Disposable cups will not be provided during the event.


<strong>Article 5 (Fairness and Consideration)</strong>

1. All participants must compete with sportsmanship and fairness.

2. If a participant encounters another participant in danger during the race, they must provide assistance to the extent possible and, when necessary, notify the race office immediately.

3. Intentional obstruction, verbal abuse, violence, or other unsportsmanlike conduct toward another participant or an event official may be grounds for disqualification.


<strong>Article 6 (Race Course and Changes)</strong>

1. The course for each race category will be announced through the official website and event information materials.

2. The course may be changed due to permits from the relevant authorities, construction, weather, natural disasters, safety reasons, or other circumstances, and the actual race distance may differ slightly as a result.

3. When necessary, the organizer may provide a revised GPX file or course information before the event or on site.

4. Trail running participants must check the course using not only the course markings but also the officially provided course information and GPS/GPX files.


<strong>Article 7 (Ranking and Awards)</strong>

1. Rankings are determined by the official timing results, and awards are presented to the top five men and top five women in each race category based on gun time.

2. There are no age-group awards.

3. Award recipients must go through identity verification. If the registered participant and the actual participant are different, the participant will be excluded from the awards.

4. Special awards may be presented separately according to the organizer&#39;s operating plan.


<strong>Article 8 (Race Number and Wearing Requirements)</strong>

1. Race numbers (bibs) are delivered to the participant by courier in advance or issued in person at the designated place and time, depending on the race category.

2. Reissue of a bib lost through the participant&#39;s negligence may be restricted.

3. During the race, participants must attach the bib issued to them in a clearly visible position on the front of their upper garment.

4. Participants may not use another person&#39;s bib, or make, alter, or transfer a bib without authorization. If a valid bib cannot be confirmed, participation or recognition of the result may be restricted.


<strong>Article 9 (Participation Gifts and Finisher Gifts)</strong>

1. Participation gifts are provided according to the specifications selected or entered at registration, and exchange on site may be restricted.

2. Participation gifts and bibs are either delivered by courier in advance or collected on site at the designated date and place, depending on the race category.

3. Finisher gifts are provided to participants who meet the finishing criteria for their race category.

4. The specific collection schedule and method will be announced in the Notice section of the website.


<strong>Article 10 (Timing and Record Management)</strong>

1. Results for timed race categories are measured using an electronic timing device.

2. The official timing method is applied according to the event operating plan, and details may be announced separately before the event.

3. If the timing device is lost or damaged, the participant must notify race headquarters immediately.

4. Trail running participants must submit their own GPS record if requested by the organizer. Manipulation of a GPS track or refusal to submit it without a valid reason may be used as grounds for verifying the result and deciding on disqualification.


<strong>Article 11 (Time Limits and Stopping the Race)</strong>

1. The time limit and cut-off time for each race category are as follows.

- 15KM TRAIL RUN: Time limit 4 hours | No intermediate cut-off

- 28KM TRAIL RUN: Time limit 7 hours 30 minutes | Cut-off of 4 hours 30 minutes (time limit) at CP2

※ The time limits for 5KM · 10KM RUNNING will be announced on the official website.

2. Participants who exceed the time limit or cut-off time must follow the race staff&#39;s instructions to stop racing, and their result will not be recognized.

3. Race staff or medical personnel may instruct a participant to stop racing if they judge that continuing is difficult for health or safety reasons, and the participant must comply.

4. A participant who voluntarily withdraws must immediately notify the race office or on-site race staff and return safely as instructed.


<strong>Article 12 (Checkpoint Operations)</strong>

1. The 28KM race has three checkpoints (CPs) on the course and the 15KM race has one checkpoint (CP). Snacks and water are provided at some checkpoints.

2. Checkpoints close in sequence as the time limit for each race category expires.

3. Waste generated on the course may be discarded only at designated checkpoints or other announced locations.


<strong>Article 13 (Course Markings and Precautions)</strong>

1. Course markings are installed at major junctions and other points to help prevent participants from leaving the course.

2. Course markings are installed in a way that minimizes damage to the natural environment and may be partly damaged by weather or external factors.

3. Participants must not damage, move, or remove course markings. Intentional damage, if confirmed, may result in disqualification.

4. Course markings are removed in sequence after the event ends.


<strong>Article 14 (Emergency Medical Services)</strong>

1. Medical personnel are stationed at the start and finish areas and provide basic first aid when necessary.

2. Depending on their condition, participants may be transported to a nearby medical facility. Medical and treatment costs incurred after transport may be borne by the participant, depending on whether the relevant insurance applies.

3. The operating hours and detailed plan for medical support may be adjusted according to on-site conditions.


<strong>Article 15 (Prohibition of Outside Assistance and Cheating)</strong>

1. In trail running events, receiving items, food, equipment, or other support for the race from outside persons in sections other than officially permitted locations may be restricted.

2. A participant who uses a vehicle or any other unauthorized means of transportation will be disqualified.

3. Mutual assistance between participants for safety is permitted.

4. If cheating is confirmed through GPS records, video, or other objective evidence, measures such as invalidation of the result, disqualification, or restrictions on participation in future events may be taken.


<strong>Article 16 (Protests and Objections)</strong>

1. Any objection regarding the race results must be filed with race headquarters within one hour after the end of the event.

2. The filing method and handling procedure follow the instructions of the on-site race headquarters.

3. Objections filed after the set period may be restricted.

2. If cancellation is requested before 18:00 (KST) on Friday, November 27, 2026, the entry fee will be refunded in full.
