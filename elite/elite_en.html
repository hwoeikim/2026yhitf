How to apply

Create your account

Sign up on this website and log in.

Submit the Elite Athlete Application

Complete the form below, including the link to your ITRA profile.

Profile review

Our team personally reviews your ITRA profile and confirms your tier.

Personal invitation

Approved athletes receive a personal invitation and an entry code. Register for your race with the code — no entry fee required.

Your athlete manager

For Tier 1 and Tier 2, a dedicated athlete manager will contact you by email to arrange your travel and accommodation.

Invitations are offered on a first-come, first-served basis. Each tier closes once all places are filled.

ITRA scores are verified at the time of application.

Airfare is booked by the organizer within a set budget. Accommodation is provided for 2 nights (single room). Approved support costs are settled after the event.

Already registered? You are welcome to apply — once approved, your entry fee will be refunded.
