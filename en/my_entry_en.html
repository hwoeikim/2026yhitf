My Registration

Look up your registration with your registration number or order number and your name.

Your registration number (YHITF-EP-… / YHITF-EG-…) is shown when you complete the registration form. Your order number (15 digits) is in MYPAGE > Order History.
For group entries, the representative's name shows all participants; a participant's own name shows only their details.

· Without an order number, your phone number, date of birth and address are partly hidden for privacy.

· To change your details, please contact us via MYPAGE > Support by 18:00 KST, Fri, Nov 27, 2026.
